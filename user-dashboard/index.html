Help & Support

Get help, browse FAQs, or submit a support ticket

🤖

Welcome to SmartWaste Support! 👋 I'm here to help with waste collection, reports, and account issues.

How do I submit a waste report?
Click Report Waste in the sidebar or the Report issue button at the top. Select a category, describe the problem, and add your location. You earn 10 green points for each verified report!
How do green points work?
You earn 10 green points for every verified waste report. Accumulate points and redeem them in Recycling Rewards for bus passes, eco bags, recycling bins, and community garden vouchers.
How do I request a custom pickup?
Go to Schedule Pickup in the sidebar, then click any future date on the calendar or press Request pickup. Choose your preferred date, time slot, and any special instructions. You'll receive a notification when it's confirmed.
What are the collection times for my zone?
Check Schedule Pickup to see your zone's regular collection days. Kabulonga: Mon/Wed/Fri. Northmead: Tue/Thu/Sat. Kanyama: Mon/Wed/Fri. Matero: Tue/Thu. Chelston: Mon/Thu/Sat. Times may vary on public holidays.
How do I track the collection truck in real time?
Navigate to Realtime Tracking in the Collection section. The live map shows truck positions, estimated arrival time, and current speed. It auto-refreshes every 10 seconds.
How do I upgrade my subscription?
Go to Settings → Subscription or Payment History → Upgrade plan. Choose Standard (ZMW 50/month) or Premium (ZMW 150/month) and pay via Airtel Money or Zanaco Bank.
What if the collection truck doesn't arrive?
Confirm your collection day in Schedule Pickup. If missed, submit a Missed collection report via Report Waste. You can also raise a support ticket on this page.
How do I contact Lusaka City Council directly?
Call +260 211 123 456 or email info@lcc.zm. Office hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00. 24-hour emergency hotline: +260 211 987 654.
